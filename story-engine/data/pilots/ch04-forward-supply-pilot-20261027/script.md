## Full script

### COLD OPEN

**FS-P01-C01** (8 s, museum-light push-in): Lights rise slowly across the empty showroom onto one matte graphite helmet with a blank clearance sticker clamp.
- **THE VOICE OF FORWARD:** From the forward depot. Forward Supply.
- _Post text:_ ost_military_boutique_l3 (post, lower third, 3 s)

**FS-P01-C02** (7 s, locked medium): Bill steps to the helmet pedestal and gives it a crisp, sincere salute, holding it one beat.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** Good evening. Tonight we ship one impossible order.

**FS-P01-C03** (8 s, locked medium): Mira joins at the podium and presses a fresh blank color sticker onto her clipboard without looking.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** Clearance tonight is Signal Mauve.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** Naturally. Signal Mauve.

### BRIEF

**FS-P01-C04** (8 s, museum-light push-in): Mira sits on the single stool in the glass booth, reading the blank amber HUD strip with total focus.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** Priority brief. Outpost Quiet requests one helmet. Delivery before the fog lifts.

**FS-P01-C05** (6 s, locked medium): Mira lowers the clipboard and looks one degree toward camera.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** The fog does not lift. That is the brief.

**FS-P01-C06** (7 s, locked medium): Tim hovers in and sets a blank sealed envelope on the podium. Bill slides it aside without opening it.
- **TIM:** *chirp* *servo click*
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** Not tonight, Tim.

### DEMO

**FS-P01-C07** (8 s, slow orbit): Slow orbit around the helmet; a thin visor slides down smoothly and glows faint cyan.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** The Quietline Helmet. Fog-rated visor. Hears what the fog is thinking.

**FS-P01-C08** (7 s, locked medium): Mira peels a mauve sticker and presses it on the helmet's clearance clamp with precision.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** Rated to Signal Mauve. Per the sticker, Commander.

**FS-P01-C09** (6 s, locked medium): Tim drifts close, its cyan optic flickers, and it chirps mid-sentence; Bill pauses, unblinking, then continues.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** Fully sealed against—
- **TIM:** *chirp chirp*
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** —signal drift.

### HOUSE AD

**FS-P01-C10** (5 s, museum-light push-in): Light rises on the plain black remote on a velvet pad; a tiny LED glows softly.
- **THE VOICE OF FORWARD:** From the house of thunder trundle. The Make A Clip Remote.
- _Post text:_ product super 'MAKE A CLIP REMOTE' (post, register as new ost id before use)

**FS-P01-C11** (5 s, locked medium): A hand presses one media key; no click sound, only the tiny board LED brightens. The TV glows softer cyan as if listening.
- **THE VOICE OF FORWARD:** Press the Make a Clip key. Say your idea. Silent press.

**FS-P01-C12** (5 s, museum-light push-in): The plain TV screen blooms into an abstract cyan clip of a sky over hills, forming from soft light particles.
- **THE VOICE OF FORWARD:** SparkNest catches it. Thunderear hears it. Clipforge builds it. Your idea, now a clip.

### BAY

**FS-P01-C13** (8 s, locked medium): Osmund jogs up through the fog carrying a bulky modular field pack and stops at attention, breathing hard.
- **DEPOT CLERK OSMUND:** Is this the priority brief?

**FS-P01-C14** (7 s, locked medium): Mira checks the pack against her clipboard; it is clearly a thermal ration pack, not a helmet case.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** That's a thermal ration pack, Osmund.
- **DEPOT CLERK OSMUND:** It was on the priority shelf.

**FS-P01-C15** (8 s, slow orbit): Bill studies the pack, opens a side panel revealing a padded helmet-shaped cavity beside sealed ration tins.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** Outpost Quiet is cold. And hungry. The pack fits the brief.

### LAUNCH

**FS-P01-C16** (8 s, museum-light push-in): Bill seats the helmet into the pack and clips it closed; Tim lowers and locks onto the pack's harness with a firm servo clamp.
- **TIM:** *low steady chirp*

**FS-P01-C17** (8 s, locked medium): The sealed hatch splits open with heavy hydraulic weight; fog pours in, amber lights strobe slow and steady.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** Hatch open. Fog holding.

**FS-P01-C18** (8 s, slow orbit): Tim lifts the pack and rises into the fog, single cyan optic cutting a clean beam, until it is a small light in the night.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** Courier away.

### ENDING

**FS-P01-C19** (8 s, locked medium): Bill and Mira back at the podium. Bill glances at the unopened envelope, then squares to camera.
- **LT. MIRA S'MIRIN':** Order shipped. Inspection status: unknown.
- **COMMANDER BILL D'BEARS:** As it should be. Let's ship it forward.

**FS-P01-C20** (5 s, museum-light push-in): A clean black card with soft cyan edge glow; space reserved center for the motto added in post.
- _Post text:_ ost_ship_it_forward: LET'S SHIP IT FORWARD (post, center)

### PRODUCTION CARD

**FS-P01-C21** (5 s, locked medium): A small brushed-graphite emblem of a stylized folded hatch turns once on a graphite surface and settles.
- **THE VOICE OF FORWARD:** A Quiet Hatch Pictures production.
- _Post text:_ production card: QUIET HATCH PICTURES (post; new ost id to register)
