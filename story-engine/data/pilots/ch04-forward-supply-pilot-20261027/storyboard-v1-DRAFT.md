## Episode spine
Tim's birthday is tomorrow and nobody at Outpost Quiet knows except Mira, who promised to make it feel like home: a sunrise, which the fog has never once allowed. At stake is Tim's quiet loneliness and Mira's promise. Meanwhile Bill keeps an unopened envelope from Command on his desk and his fuse shortens as an "urgent" helmet delivery to a station across the fog eats the day. **Turn:** Osmund's "wrong" pack and the Sky Diffuser are both needed for the delivery, forcing a choice between the order and the birthday. **Ending:** Bill opens the envelope — it's blank except the word "Tomorrow" — and decides tomorrow can wait; he gives up the delivery window, the crew paint a sunrise in the bunk room for Tim, and Bill and Tim share their first real moment. Something changes: Bill is the one who chose people over orders.
