# Episode brief: "The Efficiency Review"
Channel 4 (Military Boutique). Director, 2026-10-27, about 8:15 PM CT. Home-only draft. Nothing rendered, queued or sent. Needs trundle's yes before any paid render and a separate yes before airing.

## Logline
When a midnight efficiency review threatens to retire one host, Bill and Mira each quietly sell the other out to save themselves, only to learn the review was filed by Tim, the courier drone who has been taking notes on both of them the whole time.

## Want and stakes
- **Main character: Bill.** He wants to keep his job as the face of the floor. He's sure he's earned it, though he can't name one thing he does.
- **Stakes:** One host gets "reassigned to outbound logistics" (the loading dock, in the rain) by morning. For Bill that would mean losing his job, his title and his brass tag.
- **Opposition:** Mira wants the same thing and is better at the game. Osmund carries the paperwork, can't keep a secret and gets used by both of them.
- **Hidden player:** Tim, the drone everyone treats as furniture.

## Tone
Dry, mean, office-politics knife fight in a museum-quiet showroom. Nobody yells. Everyone smiles. No slapstick. The products are only set dressing in the background and are never mentioned or shown up close.

## 3-act beat outline (~2:30)
**Act 1: The Notice (0:00-0:45)**
1. After closing, Osmund arrives out of breath with a sealed folder: "Efficiency review. One host gets reassigned by morning."
2. Bill and Mira laugh it off together, and each one is already doing the math.
3. Bill pulls Osmund aside and quietly asks who wrote it. Osmund doesn't know, but says "it was very thorough."
4. Button: Mira, alone, peels a red sticker onto her clipboard next to a box labeled "B." (Overlay only.)

**AD POD 1: #30, #31 (priced ads, untouched)**

**Act 2: The Knives (0:45-1:45)**
5. Bill "helps" Osmund draft a staff note full of praise for Mira's "bold flexibility around timekeeping."
6. Mira "helps" Osmund draft one praising Bill's "calm, which some mistake for absence."
7. Osmund, overwhelmed, staples both notes into the same folder.
8. Rising: Bill and Mira share a long, polite, side-by-side push-in. Each one knows the other did it, and neither will say so.
9. Tim hovers behind them with his optic pulsing. Nobody looks at him.

**AD POD 2: #32, #33, #34**

**Act 3: The Turn and the Payoff (1:45-2:30)**
10. Turn: Osmund finally reads the header. The review was filed by Tim, unit-to-unit, and it's based on 400 hours of floor observation.
11. Bill and Mira slowly turn to Tim. Tim's optic blinks once.
12. Payoff: The verdict says both hosts get reassigned to outbound logistics. Osmund is promoted to floor host, effective immediately, and he's terrified.
13. Final image: In the rain-lit loading bay, Bill and Mira stand side by side holding parcels, still not speaking. Tim floats past them into the warm showroom. Overlay: "EFFICIENCY: IMPROVED."

**AD POD 3 / outro: #35, #36**
