# Channel 4 — BLACK ICE (espionage reboot, 2026-10-27)
Director, for Trundle. Home-only. Nothing rendered. Evolved from "The Ferry at Kostin Crossing" + Station Nine. Ads #30-36 air untouched as separate breaks only. Adults only. Fictional states, units, gear. No real people/brands named in any prompt. No on-screen text in any prompt (codenames/titles/timecodes are post overlays only).

## Title
**BLACK ICE**

## Logline
Winter 1971, an alternate Cold War. A retired sniper codenamed HERON is pulled back for one last job: cross a frozen border river and steal the heart of a secret under-ice listening weapon from the island station run by the man who trained her — while the handler in her ear may be selling her out.

## World
Two fictional blocs split by the frozen Kostin river. On Island Nine, the Eastern republic hides LORELEI: a prototype hydrophone array frozen into the ice that can hear a submarine, a footstep, or a whisper across forty kilometres. Whoever holds its master tape reel holds the border. Grounded heightened element: plausible 1970s acoustic tech, scaled one notch past history.

## Characters (codenames)
- **HERON — Vera Kost** (mid-40s). Ex-sniper of the disbanded border unit "Grey Reed." Ferry operator now. Want: one clean job, money to disappear. Stakes: capture means execution; failure means LORELEI hunts every crossing, including the people she ferries. Silhouette: long hooded snow smock over tight chest rig, slung suppressed bolt rifle, single orange paracord loop on the left shoulder strap.
- **LANTERN — Major Ilse Varga** (early 50s). Western handler, voice on the field radio, chain-smoking in a dim radio room. Calm, maternal, transactional. Want: the tape — or a deal. Silhouette: cardigan over uniform shirt, headset, reading glasses on a cord.
- **WOLFHOUND — Colonel Anton Reyd** (late 50s). HERON's old commander, now director of Island Nine. Courteous, tired, believes LORELEI will stop a war by hearing it coming. Want: keep the program alive — and bring HERON home to his side. Silhouette: long greatcoat over layered webbing, fur-trimmed cap, a scarred old sniper rifle he still carries.
Themes: mentor vs. student, states using soldiers as disposable parts, "the war ends but the soldiers don't."

## Locations
1. **The ferry landing (west bank)** — HERON's hut, cable ferry, ice.
2. **The ice field** — open frozen river at night, pressure ridges, fog, hydrophone stakes in the ice.
3. **Island Nine perimeter** — wire, searchlight tower, sodium lamps, guard path.
4. **LORELEI listening hall** — concrete bunker, reel-to-reel banks, green oscilloscopes, cables into a hole cut in the ice.
5. **LANTERN's radio room** — Western safehouse attic, valve radio sets, ashtrays, one lamp.

## Look bible
- **Era/mood:** early-70s cold-war espionage and paranoia cinema, grounded and patient.
- **Camera/stock:** 35mm film, Kodak 5254/5247-style negative look, vintage spherical primes 25/32/50/85mm, 24fps, natural grain, slight halation on practicals.
- **Movement:** static locked-off for stealth and dialogue (long tense quiet); slow creeping push-ins for dread; violent unstabilized handheld ONLY when violence breaks.
- **Palette:** desaturated steel blue, bone white, gunmetal, olive grey; INK-BLACK crushed shadows; ONE accent color: **signal orange** (HERON's paracord loop, the ferry lantern, LORELEI's single warning bulb). Nothing else is orange.
- **Gear design rules:** functional, layered, used. Straps, buckles, pouches, worn canvas webbing, taped joints, frayed edges, hand-repaired stitching, frost on metal. Every item has a reason. Asymmetric loadouts create distinct silhouettes (HERON = hood + long smock tails + rifle diagonal; WOLFHOUND = greatcoat + tall fur cap + rifle vertical on back). No insignia text, no logos.
- **Faces:** weathered, lined, wind-burned, natural skin, visible scars and fatigue. No beauty lighting.
- **Radio calls (codec style, grounded):** cut between HERON's tight close-up under the hood and LANTERN in her radio room; hiss, squelch, clicks. Codenames overlaid in post only.

## Episode 1 — "Ice Out" — Script (~150s story + breaks)

**ACT 1 — EXT. FERRY LANDING – DUSK**
Ice grinds. HERON, alone, oils a suppressed bolt rifle on the ferry deck. A field radio in a canvas bag crackles.
LANTERN (V.O.): [static, then warm] Heron. Lantern. Are you still breathing out there?
HERON: [not looking up] Depends who's asking.
INT. RADIO ROOM – SAME. LANTERN lights a cigarette off the last.
LANTERN: [easy] Island Nine. They've finished it. Lorelei hears everything on that river. You, your ferry, your passengers.
HERON: [beat; jaw tightens] Who runs it?
LANTERN: [long pause, exhales smoke] Wolfhound.
HERON stops oiling. Wind.
HERON: [quiet] He's dead.
LANTERN: [gentle] Then you won't mind killing him.
HERON looks across the ice. A single searchlight sweeps the far island.
**[AD BREAK 1]**

**ACT 2 — EXT. ICE FIELD / ISLAND NINE PERIMETER – NIGHT**
HERON crosses the ice on her belly, white smock, fog. Hydrophone stakes jut from the ice. She freezes beside one. Silence.
LANTERN (V.O.): [whisper, static] Lorelei can hear your heartbeat if you let it. Slow down.
HERON: [barely audible, breath held] I taught you that.
Searchlight passes over her. A GUARD on the perimeter path stops, listens, walks on. She slides under the wire.
INT. LORELEI HALL. Reels turn. One orange bulb pulses. HERON lifts the master reel. Behind her, a match flares.
WOLFHOUND: [calm, from the dark] Still crawling too fast, Vera.
She turns, rifle up. WOLFHOUND sits on a crate, unarmed, his old rifle across his knees.
**[AD BREAK 2]**

**ACT 3 — INT. LORELEI HALL – CONTINUOUS**
WOLFHOUND: [tired smile] Who sent you? Varga? [beat] She sold me Grey Reed's names in '64. Did she tell you that?
HERON: [rifle steady, voice not] You sold them.
WOLFHOUND: [rises slowly] I buried them. She signed the order. Ask her.
HERON keys the radio without lowering the rifle.
HERON: [cold] Lantern. Is it true?
INT. RADIO ROOM. LANTERN stubs out the cigarette. A second man's gloved hand sets a folder by her elbow.
LANTERN: [long pause; flat] Bring me the reel, Heron. Then we'll talk.
WOLFHOUND: [soft] That's a yes.
Outside: engines on the ice — LANTERN's own extraction team, rifles up, coming for both of them. The orange bulb flares. SUDDEN VIOLENCE: HERON shoots out the lamp; handheld chaos, muzzle flashes in black, glass, reels spilling tape.
EXT. ICE FIELD – MOMENTS LATER. HERON and WOLFHOUND, side by side, run across the ice into fog, the reel under her smock. The ice cracks behind them. Cut to black on radio static.
LANTERN (V.O.): [static] Heron... Heron, answer me.
**[END — ad pod]**
